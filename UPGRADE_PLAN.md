# Bright Hero, Upgrades, and Reunion Finale

## Visual premise

Right Shoe should read as the brightest object on the adventure route: a glossy coral high-top with polished cream rubber, warm edge lights, a visible sparkle trail, and more luminous eyes. The material treatment should feel like a freshly cleaned sneaker under a toy-room lamp, rather than a flat sprite. The upgrade path must preserve this recognizable silhouette while making each transformation immediately legible during play.

| Route moment | Upgrade | Visible change | Gameplay effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shoebox Cliff | **Coral Chrome** | Brighter coral upper, gold rim glow, glittering lace trail | Raises the visual energy of the hero and adds a brief speed flourish. |
| Laundry Lane | **Moonstep Runner** | Cobalt-and-cream running-shoe trim with wing streaks | Grants an additional aerial recovery and a faster dash recharge. |
| Lace Bridge | **Lace Lash** | Animated coral lace coil around the shoe | Sends a short-range lash forward to clear nearby shoe fiends. |
| Rogue Tower | **Gum Stomp** | Gum-sole imprint, lime-pink impact burst | Performs a ground stomp that defeats close enemies and makes a sticky bounce. |

## Finale direction

Once Right Shoe reaches Left Shoe, the win state becomes a distinct **Pair Restored** screen. Both shoes appear at the center of a warmly lit shoebox ballroom, facing one another and dancing a synchronized two-step: heel tap, pivot, lace-spin, toe bump, and a final heart-shaped pose. Coral and gold confetti, buttons, and lace curls rise behind them. The existing replay action remains available, with a direct **WATCH SUPER RUN AGAIN** option when the AI route finished the rescue.

## Interaction and readability

The active HUD should display the currently earned transformation as a small stitched patch and show two ability chips, **LACE LASH** and **GUM STOMP**, only after their corresponding pickups are collected. On keyboard, abilities can use `Q` and `E`; touch controls expose them as short labeled buttons. The deterministic Super Run should acquire each upgrade in sequence and use the powers visibly against enemies so the mode functions as a complete showcase.
