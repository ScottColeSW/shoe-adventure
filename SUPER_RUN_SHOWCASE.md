# Super Run Power Showcase

## Objective

Turn the Super Run into a legible, celebratory action demonstration where the player can clearly see Right Shoe’s movement, upgrades, abilities, escalating combat, and reunion payoff.

## Showcase Route

| Beat | Route Moment | Player Read | Reward / Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coral launch | Fully opaque, luminous Right Shoe leaves the start | Hero silhouette is immediately readable against the navy room |
| 2 | Double-jump gate | Two deliberate jumps cross a raised lace ramp | Wingtip Feather grants **Super Jump** |
| 3 | Bonus capture | Super Jump reaches a high bonus patch | Bonus buttons and bright capture burst |
| 4 | Weapon lane | Lace Lash and Gum Stomp patches appear with explicit callouts | Player gains both combat abilities |
| 5 | Mini-boss pair | Lash clears the lace goblin; Gum Stomp defeats a rogue skate | Each move receives a distinct action line and particle effect |
| 6 | Ultra Move charge | Hero collects the Ultra Sole core near the rescue tower | A large glowing charge state is visible in the HUD and hero halo |
| 7 | Boss encounter | The Boss blocks the final spool lift | Ultra Move defeats it and opens the rescue lane |
| 8 | Rescue and reunion | Right Shoe reaches Left Shoe | A five-to-seven-second dancing finale appears before replay options |

## Visibility Requirements

The hero’s source art can retain transparent pixels around the shoe, but the in-game character must have a fully opaque, high-contrast core silhouette. The procedural shoe body will remain visible under the billboard, and the billboard material will use an opaque, emissive presentation rather than a low-alpha composite. The hero halo will remain behind the shoe rather than washing through it.

## Timing Requirements

The Super Run should reserve enough screen time for every named moment. Action messaging must not overwrite the just-earned upgrade immediately. The dancing reunion screen will use a six-second preview window before exposing its replay actions, with progress copy that tells viewers how long the celebration lasts.

## Originality Constraint

Contraptions and encounters remain original household-mechanical creations in the shoe-rescue universe; the update must not copy any other game’s characters, board layout, or art.
