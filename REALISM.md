# Realistic Footwear Direction

The upgrade preserves the game’s rescue-story clarity while giving each shoe a more believable product-render presence. The **Right Shoe** will become a coral-red children’s high-top sneaker with visible stitched leather panels, a cream rubber sole, threaded eyelets, rounded laces, and a subtle animated eye band integrated into the upper rather than pasted on. The **Left Shoe** will match this construction in a warm cream-and-coral palette so the pair reads as intentionally made for one another.

The main shoe enemy will become a scuffed midnight-blue roller skate with realistic suede and plastic textures, four translucent wheels, and one restrained mischievous eye band. The mood stays handcrafted and friendly: natural material grain, warm lamp highlights, contact shadows, and clear silhouettes take priority over photorealism that would blur platforming readability.

All upgraded assets will be transparent-background, three-quarter side-profile character renders with no text, no brand logos, no extra limbs, and composition safe for the game’s left-to-right side-scrolling stage.
